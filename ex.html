Before every session
  • Warm up for 5 minutes: march in place, arm circles, gentle hip and ankle circles.
  • Finish each set with 2–3 reps still in reserve. Do not train to failure.
  • Breathe out on the effort. Never hold your breath in planks and bridges.
  • Do balance work next to a wall or chair.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort. With a heart, blood pressure or joint condition, check with your doctor first.
